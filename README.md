# Carolina Rauber — Business Analytics Portfolio

A responsive portfolio presenting Carolina da Costa Rauber’s business analytics projects in forecasting, customer insights, business strategy and data architecture.

## Preview

Open `index.html` in a browser. No installation or build step is required. Google Fonts requires an internet connection; system fonts provide a fallback.

## Features

- Seven case studies with expandable findings, methods and recommendations
- Filters by analytical focus
- Sales forecasting and confidence interval visualisations
- Responsive layouts and keyboard-accessible controls
- Email and LinkedIn contact links

## GitHub Pages

In the repository, open **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**, then choose **main** and **/ (root)** and save.

## Content

Projects are primarily academic studies based on real business situations. Forecasts and recommendations are not presented as realised outcomes. Methodological limitations are retained in the case studies.
